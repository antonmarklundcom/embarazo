# D0 design decisions — 2026-09-20

**Chosen.** Brand-exact with the app: cream ground, petrol structure, terracotta as the one CTA colour, five pastel tints as the clustering system, Nunito Sans for display *and* body (a second serif was offered and declined — editorial voice comes from scale, not a second face). Split hero with a phone frame, bento on the home and `/app`, big-type hubs, timeline for trimesters.
**Rejected.** Glass, marquee, count-ups, parallax, carousels, pop-ups, cookie banners, email capture, forms. Dark mode: the brand has no dark palette and inventing one would drift from the app, so `color-scheme: light` is declared rather than left to the browser.

**Two AA deviations from the app's tokens,** both darker, both in `tokens.css`: `--muted` #6E675D (the app's #7A7369 is 4.39:1 on cream) and `--accent-text` #B24A2A (white on #C96342 is 3.93:1; #C96342 on cream is 3.68:1 — the CTA is the one element that must never be hard to read). Brand terracotta #C96342 survives untouched as `--accent` for every non-text use.
**Derived rule, now in the token file:** terracotta text only on cream or white — on the pastel tints it falls to 4.0–4.3:1, so text on a tint is `--petrol` or `--tone-arena-text`. This also makes terracotta mean exactly one thing: *this goes to the app*. Focus rings are blue on purpose; a terracotta ring on a terracotta button is invisible.

**No JavaScript except the calculator.** The nav sheet, the FAQ accordion, the `/instalar` OS switch and the *dismissible* sticky bar are `<details>` plus one hidden checkbox. So `/instalar` does not device-detect: Android opens by default (the PY majority), iPhone is one tap away. S1 may add a ~10-line progressive enhancement to open the right one; the page must work without it.

**Phone frame is CSS, not an image.** It renders the app's real week-20 screen in HTML: zero bytes, zero CLS, real numbers today, and it stays as the permanent fallback. When real screenshots arrive (human-inputs #10), `partials/phone-frame.php` swaps the mock for an `<img>` of the same size. The home hero ships with the tinted size panel as the *design*, not as a placeholder.

**Bento mobile order = source order;** desktop changes only spans. 1 Semana a semana (wide) · 2 Herramientas · 3 Derechos · 4 Emergencia · 5 Familia por WhatsApp — decreasing value for someone arriving from a symptom search: the week she is in first, the thing that rescues her second.

**CTA placement per page type.** Product pages (`/`, `/app`, `/instalar`, `/familia`, `/sobre`) carry the hard CTA in the hero and again at the close, and **no sticky bar** — it would compete with the hero. Content pages (weeks, articles, hubs, blog) carry no CTA above the H1, a contextual `cta-week`/`cta-tool` after the first substantive section, a second before the FAQ, and **one** sticky bar: dismissible, hidden ≥ 720 px, with a spacer reserving its height so it never covers the footer. Tool pages put the island first and the hand-off inside the result, never before it. The trust strip only ever sits beside a CTA, never as or near the H1.

**Two deliberate restraints.** The Guaraní strip is built and shown in `components.html` but absent from `articulo.html`: §1.8 limits it to the emergency article and the rights hub, and a preview is not a reason to bend that. And the app plan's P-3 title is 69 characters, so `articulo.html` uses "Licencia de maternidad en Paraguay: semanas, inicio y pago" (58) and moves "(Ley 5508)" into the H1 and description.

**`/design-sync` was not available.** The `DesignSync` tool exists here but returns "needs design-system authorization", and `/design-login` cannot run non-interactively. To push the library to a claude.ai/design project named "Mi Bebé — embarazo.com.py", run `/design-login` once in an interactive Claude Code session on this machine, then re-run the sync. The files in `design/` are the deliverable either way — T0 and T1 read them, not the design project.

**Verified before commit,** at 360 px and 1024 px in Chromium: heading outline in order with one `h1` per page, no dead links or anchors, no duplicate ids, no horizontal overflow, no console errors, every tap target ≥ 44 px, every text node ≥ WCAG AA against its actually-painted background. The calculator returns semana 29 · 28 semanas y 6 días · FPP 7 de diciembre de 2026 for FUM 2026-03-02 on 2026-09-20, and reproduces the app's own unit-test vector (121 días → 17 semanas y 2 días).
